How to playTake your turn whenever you like — the game waits for you. When you end your turn, send the short link to the next player. Your game stays saved on this device, so you can leave and come back. Tap one of your units, then a white dot to move or a red tile to attack. Walk onto a village to capture it (+2 stars per village each turn). Tap an empty village of yours to train units. Units heal on your villages and forests give defence. Tap a fruit, animal or fish next to your village to harvest it (2 stars): fed villages level up for more income, a workshop and a defensive wall. Clouds hide unexplored land: your units and villages reveal what is nearby. The tinted tiles around a village show its range. Tap Tech tree to spend stars on four branches of technology (economy, cavalry, defence, warfare) — Riders and Defenders must be researched before you can train them. The map is large: scroll to pan it and use − / + to zoom. Capture every enemy village to win.
